Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

What we collect

TapDuel may process your Google account identifier, display name, email address, game profile, match results, reaction times, virtual inventory, device/browser technical data, and information you submit through sponsor/contact forms.

Why we use it

We use this information to provide login, multiplayer matchmaking, leaderboards, virtual rewards, fraud prevention, customer support, product analytics, and sponsor enquiries.

Service providers

TapDuel currently uses Firebase for authentication/database services, Render for backend hosting, and Vercel for frontend hosting. Their own privacy terms also apply.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Public leaderboards may display your chosen username and game statistics. We may disclose information when required by law or to protect the service.

Retention and deletion

We retain account and match records while needed to operate the service. To request account deletion or correction, use the contact form on TapDuel and provide the email used to sign in.

Children

Users should meet the minimum age required to use Google services in their country. TapDuel is not designed to collect information from children below that age.

Contact

Use the sponsor/contact form on the main TapDuel website for privacy requests.
