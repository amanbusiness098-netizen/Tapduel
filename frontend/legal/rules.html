Game & Fair Play Rules

Quick Match / Private Room

Wait for the countdown and the TAP NOW signal. Tapping before the valid signal loses the match. The server records the first valid tap it receives. A disconnect or forfeit after the active signal can award the opponent the win.

Solo Challenge

Wait until TAP NOW. Tapping early ends the attempt. Only plausible valid results are saved. A limited number of Solo runs per day can award coins.

Connection

Keep the page visible and use a stable connection. Device and network differences can affect results.

Prohibited behaviour

No bots, macros, scripts, request manipulation, multiple-account farming, harassment, or exploitation. Suspicious results may be removed.

Money

There are no player deposits, cash stakes, cash withdrawals, or player-funded prize pools.
